Cold & dark to takeoff (FCOM normal procedures, simplified)

ELEC: BAT 1, BAT 2, EMER BAT 1/2 ON → EXT 1/2 ON (GPU from J) · ADIRS 1/2/3 NAV · APU MASTER, then APU START · APU BLEED ON · FUEL pumps ON · MFD: INIT (flight number), F-PLN, FUEL&LOAD, PERF T.O (compute V1/VR/V2, FLEX) · Doors closed, stairs removed · BEACON ON · ENG MODE IGN/START · ENG MASTER 2 then 1 · ENG MODE NORM · APU BLEED OFF · FLAPS 1 (1+F) · GND SPLRS ARM · Auto brake RTO · T.O CONFIG test · Taxi with the tiller · Thrust levers FLX (or TO/GA) · Rotate at VR to ~12.5°, positive climb → gear up · thrust reduction altitude → CL detent.
